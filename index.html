Welcome to Aamrai

A Place to Feel Valued, Cared For & At Home.

Project Aamrai is a peaceful senior living and care initiative nestled amidst nature in Savarde, Sangli. Surrounded by greenery and mango trees, Aamrai aims to provide a safe, comfortable and caring environment where senior citizens can live with dignity, companionship and peace of mind.

About Aamrai

Peaceful Living, Closer to Nature

1 Acre Mango Orchard

Aamrai is envisioned as a peaceful and caring living space for senior citizens who wish to spend their days in a safe, comfortable and natural environment.

Located in the serene surroundings of Savarde, Sangli, Aamrai combines a homely atmosphere with the calmness of nature.

The property is surrounded by approximately one-acre mango orchard, creating a calm and refreshing environment away from the city noise and stress.

Meet the Founder

Prachi Gavhane

Founder, Project Aamrai

Project Aamrai was born from a simple belief — every senior citizen deserves a life filled with care, respect, companionship and peace.

The vision is to create more than just a place to stay: a warm, safe and homely environment where senior citizens can enjoy their everyday lives surrounded by nature, greenery and meaningful companionship.

With the serene beauty of an aamrai (mango orchard) as its setting, Aamrai aims to bring together the comfort of home and the calmness of nature.

“Aamrai is not just a place to live; it is a place to feel valued, cared for and at home.”
— Founder, Project Aamrai
